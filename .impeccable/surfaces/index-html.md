# Surface brief: Η εβδομάδα μου (index.html)

Scope: the whole planner surface (hero, header, course rail, week, stats, checks, back pages, dialogs, print). Mode: Operate, with a Persuade-grade first viewport.
Audience/job: ΤΗΜΜΥ students (owner + classmates) picking courses and checking a conflict-free, rule-aware week before registration.
Constraints: GitHub Pages static build and print/PDF must keep working; every existing feature, id and Greek copy stays; the standalone THMMY-programma.html may drift. Scripts, styles, fonts, pictures and the film stay self-hosted (CSP).
Requested: motion graphics, 3D and visuals that stun without costing performance, modelled on butter.video. History: the Engineering Pad world was rejected, then the "Premium product app" world (cool greys, electric blue, pastel courses, Commissioner) was rejected for its colours, button shapes and format. The owner then ordered a revamp as a flat poster, which is the current direction; a blue field for the winter semester, tried first, was rejected by the owner and does not return. The user chose "Butter + live 3D week", palette "Butter mono + neon", layout "Week-first canvas", and asked for a fitting Greek-capable font (Geologica).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A flat poster for the week: the student's real timetable is the hero object (glossy 3D slabs on a floating board, a chrome sample week while empty) standing on a field of the season's colour, the planner sheet rises over it, the working surface below is calm, editorial and week-first, and back pages carry the page to its end. Refuses the cluttered university-portal look and any costume metaphor.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral greys (a #ededed / #0b0b0b page ground) with an off-white or ink planner sheet, and a flat field per season over the first screen: winter #ffd23f in both themes, the yellow of the university's building on the Volos quay (the former Papastratos tobacco warehouses), spring #a7ff9c, or #0a6436 with light ink in the dark theme. The field and the sheet meet at a seam, with no gradient, glow or shadow between them. Ink type, one neon accent (hot pink #ef1a84 / #ff4fa8) for the headline period, today, highlights and one bar on the back pages. Vivid course colours (cobalt, hot pink, tangerine, violet, aqua, lemon, sky, orchid, periwinkle), assigned per course by default; no grey course colour. Semantic success / warning / danger for passed / review / overlap, always paired with an icon, dashes or an outline. Geologica for all type with tabular figures, on a small scale: 13 for labels, 15 for controls and notes, 16 for planner text, 18 for back-page paragraphs, 20 and 24 for leads and headings, and three display sizes. Pills for everything pressable; 20-28px soft surfaces; 12px event blocks that are flat fields of the course colour (no lip, rim, inner edge or resting shadow). Flat at rest: surfaces part by a step of tone, controls are a flat step of ink (--fill, hover --fill-2) with no border or shadow, and outside the calendar grid nothing is divided by a hairline; only floating things (dialogs, menus, drawer, tooltip, toast) cast a shadow, and a hovered block lifts to one. Back pages are flat panels meeting edge to edge, with real pictures (the planner's own screens and found photographs, credited where they stand). The page ends on a black field in both themes. No glass anywhere; the dialog backdrop is the one frost.

STORY: A student lands on their own week as an object, scrolls and the board tilts flat while the planner sheet rises, adds courses from a rail that is always within reach (docked, drawer, or phone sheet) until nothing is red, then prints one A4 sheet. Further down, the page shows how it works, where the hours come from and who made it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A skip link first for the keyboard, then a plain fixed header bar, in the field's own colour over the hero (brand, profile, theme, data menu, print). Composed as a poster on the season's flat field. The two-line display headline "Η εβδομάδα μου." sits top-left at poster size (weight 800) on the sheet's own left edge, its first line running from margin to margin; the one-sentence sub (what the chosen week holds right now, or the invitation to choose courses) and the ink pill CTA "Στο πρόγραμμα" sit at the foot, just above the sheet's edge, in front of the 3D stage. The Three.js week board stands under the headline, right of its short second line and out past the right edge, down to the sheet, whose edge cuts it (between the headline and the sub, at full width, on phones, where it stays under the sheet and a band of the field's colour goes up under the sub and the action); it reacts to the cursor, shows a solid surface tooltip card (lifted shadow) on hover and opens the course dialog on click. The planner sheet's toolbar (season switch, refresh as a fill pill) peeks at the bottom.

FORM: user-reference-led, no seed key. The user pointed at https://www.butter.video/ ("i also meant this") and then picked, in their own words and choices: "Butter + live 3D week", palette "Butter mono + neon", layout "i wanna pick the fist option so Week-first canvas" plus "find a fitting font". Butter-derived studio (Lenis smooth scroll, Three.js hero, giant grotesk, scroll-linked rise, plain fixed nav) applied to an Operate surface. Signature motion: motion has depth along the line of sight, so things arrive from and leave toward the viewer's side of the glass and always resolve to the flat, exact planner; line-mask headline reveal, each line lying back and standing up toward the reader; board rise from deep in the studio (a wide lens that closes to a long one as it lands, a dolly zoom) with its slabs dealt onto it; the tiles are objects and the board their tray, so whenever the week changes place or content the tiles leave the tray and are dealt back Monday to Friday with one landing, each coming in from the viewer's side of the glass and shrinking onto its slot (scrolling down lifts them and deals them into the planner, each becoming its block; scrolling back up throws them at the viewer and out past the edges of the screen, and they come back from beyond the edge onto the board; a hard spin by hand flings them onto the studio floor and they hop back; a season switch re-deals the week, replacing the earlier 3D page turn at the user's request); scroll tilts the camera top-down and lands the board on the week as the sheet rises (the hero is not pinned, and scrolls away with the page); the header's solid sheet-coloured ground fades in as the planner docks, with no edge line; odometer stats; a course flies on an arc from its row into its slot, swelling toward the viewer on the way; course dialog grows out of the clicked block, and dialogs come forward out of the page; the page ends on a black field where the title is rebuilt from blocks in the week's colours, which come up from under their baseline one after another as the page's end scrolls in; the back pages' steps row opens a panel by view transition for a pointer (a key just changes it), and their parts rise in as they scroll into view. In the hero the board is a free-view object: drag to orbit (pitch limited above the board), pinch or Ctrl+wheel to zoom within a range, double-click to reset; it glides home as soon as the page scrolls, and the headline, sub and button stay in front of it. Slab height encodes how often a course meets in the week. Everything collapses to a static, instant path under reduced motion.

LAYOUT RULES: The week never scrolls sideways. Five equal day columns whatever they hold; parallel lessons split their day into lanes, and narrow lanes step down from start time plus the name, soft-hyphenated only at this width (112px or less), to start time plus course code (80px or less, or a lesson of an hour or less), to the code (60px or less) to the course number without the shared ECE prefix (50px or less), with the full name in the tooltip and dialog; tablets get the full-width week with the rail as a side drawer; phones get a vertical day-by-day agenda with the rail as a bottom sheet that drags or flicks down from its handle or heading. The "Μαθήματα" pill with the selected count (the courses button: sticky at the foot of the screen as the workspace's last child, flat, a 4px band of the sheet's colour around it instead of a shadow) opens the rail from anywhere while the week is in view. Selected rail rows take an 11% wash of their course colour; rows, stat cells (numbers captioned underneath), legend, checks and footer are parted by space, not lines. A full-bleed black field closes the page, the title built from blocks above the footer line; the hero copy, the sheet, the back pages and the footer share one page margin (--gutter). The hero is not pinned and makes no stacking context: its copy (z-index 3) stands in front of the fixed 3D stage (z-index 2); on phones and under reduced motion the stage lies under the sheet. The back pages are three `.poster` panels between the planner and the footer (steps: five panels in tints of the field, one open at a time, Δες with the film open at first; place: a photograph of the university's building and a pink bar with the source and the last refresh; cards: three postcards of the town on the field). They are left out of print and of the standalone HTML copy.
