# Design System: Η εβδομάδα μου · ΤΗΜΜΥ

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Week in the Studio"**

The student's own timetable is a product shot. It sits as glossy 3D slabs on a floating board in a neutral photo studio, lit like an object on a seamless backdrop, and the planner sheet rises over it like a page slid onto the set. Everything around the object stays quiet: studio greys, ink type, one neon accent. The colour lives in the courses themselves. The first screen is composed as a poster: the title sits at the top left at poster size, in the heaviest weight, on the same left edge as the sheet's content; the week stands right of its short second line and from half-way down its long first one, out past the right edge and down to the sheet, whose edge cuts it; and the week's facts and the one action sit at the foot, just above the sheet's edge. The grounds are flat: the studio is one tone and the sheet another, meeting at a seam, with no glow behind the board and no shadow on the sheet's edge. The page closes where it began, on a black field from edge to edge.

Below the fold the studio becomes a calm, editorial work surface. The week is the canvas and is never cropped sideways. Pills for everything you press, soft 20-28px surfaces, 12px event blocks that are flat fields of colour (the 3D tile is the object, the block is its print), giant heavy grotesk numerals for the stats. The signature move is a scroll morph: on desktop and tablet the 3D board swings to a top-down view and lands exactly on the planner's week, and the slabs become its flat blocks. The tiles are objects and the board is their tray: whenever the week changes place or content, the tiles leave the tray and are dealt back Monday to Friday (by day, then start time), 40ms apart and the whole deal held to about half a second, with one landing. In 3D a tile comes in from the viewer's side of the glass, large and close, and shrinks onto its slot, accelerating under gravity, and squashes as it lands; on the 2D week a block arrives a touch larger and presses down to size. The intro, the hop back after a spin, the return after a throw, the scroll down onto the planner, new blocks and the season switch all deal this way. Elsewhere motion is physical and springy: digits roll, the season thumb and blocks moving to a new slot settle with a small overshoot, and a course flies on an arc into its slot, swelling toward the viewer on the way. Exits are shorter than entrances and leave the way things came, on the ease-out: dialogs drop and fade, phone sheets slide back down, a removed block lifts away where it stood, the update toast lifts away. The one exception: a removed block's opacity runs on the CSS `ease` keyword so the fade stays readable, while its scale stays on the ease-out. A block that has just started to clash pulses its red ring once on the there-and-back curve. Every one of these collapses to an instant, static path under reduced motion.

The world is modelled on butter.video, chosen by the user. Rejected and not to return: the cool-grey, electric-blue, pastel-course "premium product app" world set in Commissioner with 16/10 radii.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Neutral studio greys with an off-white (light) or ink (dark) planner sheet; no tinted neutrals.
- Ink is the primary action colour; hot pink is a single neon note, never a fill for large surfaces.
- Nine vivid course hues, full-strength on event blocks, with ink or white text chosen by contrast.
- Geologica everywhere, tabular figures by default, display sizes in the heaviest weight (800) with tight negative tracking.
- Pill controls, soft rectangles for surfaces, 12px for event blocks.
- Surfaces part by a step of tone, not by lines or resting shadows; only things that really float (dialogs, menus, drawers, tooltips, the rail button, the toast) cast a shadow. Event blocks are flat fields of colour. The calendar grid keeps its lines.
- A poster first screen: the title at the top left at poster size on the sheet's left edge, the 3D week right of its short second line and out past the right edge, the facts and the one action at the foot. The studio and the sheet are two flat tones meeting at a seam, with no gradient, glow or shadow between them.
- The page closes on a black field in both themes, where the title is rebuilt from blocks in the week's own colours.
- The hero's 3D week and the planner's 2D week are one object: scrolling morphs one into the other.
- One landing: tiles and blocks are dealt Monday to Friday, 40ms apart, the whole deal held to about half a second; a 3D tile closes from the viewer's side of the glass under gravity and squashes, a 2D block presses from 1.1 to size.
- Motion has depth along the line of sight: things arrive from, and leave toward, the viewer's side of the glass (a tile shrinking onto its slot, a throw that comes at the lens, a dialog coming forward out of the page, a headline standing up toward the reader), and nothing just slides in the screen plane; it always resolves to the flat, exact planner.
- Three curves: the spring (~5% overshoot) for things that settle into a new value or slot, the ease-out for transitions, presses and exits, the there-and-back in-out for one-shot pulses; everything static under reduced motion.

## Colors

A monochrome studio with one neon voice; all saturation is reserved for courses.

### Primary
- **Neon Hot Pink** (`neon-pink`, dark `dark-neon-pink`): the headline's and the sign-off's full stop, today's day pill, the today column wash (5% mix), the course "add" hover (fine pointers only), the rail button's count badge, link hover underlines, selection and caret. Text on it is always ink (`on-accent`). Its hover step is `neon-pink-hover`.

### Neutral
- **Studio Grey** (`studio-grey` / `dark-studio-grey`): the page's own ground, under the hero and the sheet.
- **Studio Floor** (`studio-floor` / `dark-studio-floor`): the hero's flat ground, one tone for the whole studio behind the 3D board; the sheet meets it at a seam, with no gradient, glow or shadow between them.
- **Close** (`close` / `dark-close`): the black field the page ends on, edge to edge in both themes, carrying light type (`#f2f2f2`, `#bdbdbd` and `#a3a3a3` for text, its second step and meta); the sign-off's letters are cut out of their blocks in this colour.
- **Planner Sheet** (`planner-sheet` / `dark-planner-sheet`): the sheet that rises over the hero; also the text colour on ink pills.
- **Surfaces** (`surface`, `surface-2`, `surface-3` and dark counterparts): raised cards, dialogs and menus (`surface`); input wells, callouts, option rows, the checks panel and the empty state (`surface-2`); tags, skeleton peaks and hovered option rows (`surface-3`).
- **Fills** (`--fill`, `--fill-2`: ink at 7% / 12%, 9% / 15% in dark): the resting and hovered fill of every default and quiet control, the season track, the count chip and the add buttons, so a control is a step of ink over whatever it sits on.
- **Hairlines** (`hairline`, `hairline-strong`): the calendar's day columns, hour rules and day-head edge, input and select wells, and unchecked swatches. Nothing else is divided by a line.
- **Ink** (`ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`): text, primary buttons, the brand mark, the season thumb, focus rings. Ink-3 is the floor for meta text.

### Course palette
Nine vivid hues assigned per course by default, cycling without repeats among selected courses: cobalt, hot pink, tangerine, violet, aqua, lemon, sky, orchid, periwinkle. Blocks are flat fields of the hue at full strength, with no lip, rim, inner edge or shadow at rest (the 3D tile is the object, the block is its print). In dark mode the hue is mixed 90% with the surface. Text on a block is ink or white, whichever gives higher WCAG contrast against the fill it sits on: the hue in light mode, the 90% mix in dark mode. In the 3D hero the same hues are darkened to 90% lightness under a full clearcoat and lit like acrylic (the tile's own colour gathers at its edges); as the board lands, each slab top shifts to its block's exact flat fill so the hand-over is seamless. An empty week shows chrome sample slabs.

### Semantic
- **Success** (passed course), **Warning** (needs review), **Danger** (overlap). Each has a soft companion at 7-12% alpha for callout fills.

### Named Rules
**The One Neon Rule.** Hot pink is the only accent and it appears as small marks (a full stop, a day pill, a count, a hover), never as a button fill or a section background. Primary actions are ink.

**The Colour Is the Course Rule.** Saturation belongs to the course palette. Chrome stays grey and ink; no course is ever grey, and no course hue is pure green, amber or red, so courses can never be mistaken for a state. A selected rail row borrows its course's hue as an 11% wash, the one place chrome takes a course colour, so the list and the week read as one.

**The Never Colour Alone Rule.** Every semantic state carries a second signal: passed is a filled green swatch plus a check icon; review is a dashed amber outline plus a hatched strip; overlap is a solid red outline plus a warning icon.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geologica (self-hosted Greek and Latin subsets, variable 300-800), with Noto Sans and system-ui as fallback (Noto Sans is only named in the stack; it is not bundled)
**Body Font:** Geologica
**Label/Mono Font:** Geologica; a monospace face appears only in the transfer-code textarea

**Character:** One geometric grotesk with Greek built in, used at every size. Display sizes are in the family's heaviest weight (800) and tightly tracked; working sizes stay plain and small, so the numbers and the headline do the shouting.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(64px, min(11.6vw, 21svh), 190px), 0.86, -0.045em): the two-line hero headline only, set at poster size at the top left on the sheet's own left edge (`--gutter`), yielding to a short window (21svh) so the facts and the button never slide under the sheet, revealed line by line from masks, each line lying back (rotated 64 degrees about its baseline under 900px perspective) and standing up toward the reader as it rises, and pulled 0.05em left so the capital's side bearing lines up with the sub and the CTA. **Display phone** (800, clamp(48px, 15.2vw, 76px), same leading and tracking) takes over at 860px and below.
- **Stat** (800, clamp(44px, 5.8vw, 92px), 1.2, -0.045em): the four week totals, rendered as odometer digits; **Stat phone** (46px) at 860px and below.
- **Headline** (600, 24px, 1.2, -0.03em): dialog titles.
- **Title large** (600, 22px, 1.2, -0.025em): the empty-state title.
- **Title** (600, 20px, 1.25, -0.025em): panel headings (course rail, weekly schedule).
- **Title small** (600, 15px, 1.3): the checks summary and dialog subheads.
- **Lede** (400, clamp(17px, 1.4vw, 20px), 1.5): the hero sub-sentence (the week's facts once courses are chosen), held to 40ch; 15px on phones.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.5; paragraphs 1.6): default text with tabular figures.
- **Control** (550, 13px, 1.3): buttons, selects, menu items, the season switch.
- **Event title** (600, 13px, 1.25): course names inside blocks; time lines 12px 600, rooms and teachers 12px. Narrow lanes set the course code at 12px, step it to 11px at 60px or less (the one place text goes under 12px, because the lane has no room) and run it down the lane at 34px or less.
- **Meta** (400, 12.5px): course meta, sync time, notes, tooltips; footers 13px.
- **Label** (600, 12px): tags, badges, counters, day-head meta, hour axis. Sentence case, never uppercase. The brand mark's two-line monogram (11px 700) is a logo, not text, and is the only other exception to the 12px floor.
- **Print ramp** (print only): title 22px (followed by the season and the data's date at 13px in ink-3), stats 20px, day names 11px, event titles 9px, event times, rooms and day meta 8px. Teachers are dropped.

### Named Rules
**The Numbers Are the Headline Rule.** Figures are tabular everywhere, and the week's totals are set at display scale, not tucked into small chips.

**The One Family Rule.** Geologica carries every role; hierarchy comes from size, weight and tracking, never from a second display face.

## Layout

A week-first canvas inside a sheet. The hero is a full-viewport sticky studio (100svh, min 600px; 92svh on phones); the planner sheet overlaps it by 13svh (9svh on phones) and carries everything else. Content tracks a 2080px maximum with fluid 2vw margins: one token, `--gutter` (at least 20px, 16px on phones), is the page margin of the hero copy, the sheet's padding and the footer's bleed.

- **Desktop (>1180px):** a 340px sticky course rail beside the week, parted from it by 32px of space. On wide layouts the rail can be hidden so the week runs full-width, with the floating rail button to bring it back.
- **Tablet (861-1180px):** the week takes the full width; the rail becomes a 340px side drawer floating 12px from the edges. It slides in over 500ms and out in 200ms, and tabbing out of it closes it.
- **Phone (<=860px):** the week becomes a vertical day-by-day agenda with sticky day headings; the rail becomes a bottom sheet (84dvh, grab handle, scrim; up in 500ms, down in 200ms) that a finger can drag down from its handle or heading, closing past 80px or on a quick downward flick (over 0.5px/ms at release) and otherwise springing back; dialogs become bottom sheets; the sheet corners drop to the phone sheet radius.
- A floating ink "Μαθήματα" pill with the selected count opens the rail from anywhere once the planner is in view.
- **First screen:** the hero copy is a flex column between the header and the sheet, from 5svh under the header bar (2svh on phones) down to 36px above the sheet's edge (26px on phones): the title at the top, the facts and the one action pushed to the foot. On wide screens the 3D week fills the free space right of the title's short second line; on phones it stands between the title and the facts at full width.

Day headings (name, meta, today pill) are centred in their columns and reserve exactly two lines of meta (2.7em) so every day name aligns. Today's meta puts "Σήμερα" on its own line above "N συναντήσεις · N ώρες". On the phone agenda, headings are a left-aligned row and the word runs inline with a comma. Phones and print drop the two-line reservation.

Spacing runs on 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 22 / 28px; panels breathe at 22px vertical padding, the hour grid is 56px per hour, and the header bar is 76px (68px on phones).

### Named Rules
**The No Sideways Week Rule.** The week never scrolls horizontally. The grid is a 54px hour axis (headed Ώρα) plus five equal day columns, whatever they hold. Parallel lessons split their day into lanes, and narrow lanes step down by container width. At 112px or less a lane shows its start time and the name, set compact with soft hyphens at Greek syllable breaks; wider lanes never hyphenate and wrap whole words. At 80px or less, and for lessons of an hour or less, it shows start time plus course code. At 60px or less it shows the code only. At 50px or less the shared ECE prefix drops and only the course number remains. The full name always stays in the tooltip and dialog. Margin notes move beside the week only when the busiest day still gets 150px per lane. There the checks are a sticky margin note on a `surface-2` panel that mirrors the rail, always open, with their summary as a heading rather than a toggle (out of the tab order).

**The Print Is Its Own Layout Rule.** Print is one A4 landscape sheet in the selected theme: chrome hidden, the sheet and blocks exactly as on screen (light or dark), no today marking, the title followed by the season and the data's date, the week scaled to fit and forced fully visible, the print type ramp, all motion, shadows and focus rings off. Nothing on the printed page sizes itself with viewport units, because Chrome's print preview resolves them against the browser window and a window-tall page spills a blank second sheet.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat at rest: controls, callouts, rows, panels and event blocks part by tone, with no shadow and no outline. Only surfaces that really float take the soft layered shadows and a 1px top highlight in light mode; on ink, where shadows vanish, a hairline marks their edge instead. The planner sheet over the hero casts none: the studio and the sheet are two flat tones that meet at a seam, with no top line, shadow, gradient or glow, and the sheet's rounded top corners are its whole edge. Real depth lives in the hero, where a physically lit 3D board sits on a shadow-catching floor. Lighting is tuned per theme: in light mode a strong key from the upper left with a soft rim, in dark mode a softer key with a stronger cool rim. The floor shadow is 24% in light mode and 60% in dark mode. The board carries hairline edge lines. In light mode it is near-white and clearcoated; in dark mode it is graphite, rougher, with little clearcoat. The 3D stage is its own fixed layer above the planner sheet. It is clipped away over the sheet's toolbar and stats band and over the docked rail, so the board slides under the planner's chrome. There is no glass: the header is a plain bar and the hero tooltip a solid `surface` card with the lifted shadow. The dialog backdrop is the one frost (8px blur).

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lifted** (`0 12px 28px -12px #0e0e0e2e, 0 2px 6px -2px #0e0e0e14`): hovered event blocks, the hero tooltip, a block in flight.
- **Floating** (`0 32px 64px -24px #0e0e0e40, 0 6px 16px -6px #0e0e0e17`): dialogs, menus, the rail drawer, the rail button, the update toast, and the shadow a dealt block presses down from.
- **Highlight** (`inset 0 1px 0 #ffffffcc`): paired with floating surfaces.

### Named Rules
**The Press, Don't Lift Rule.** Controls press (scale 0.96 on active) and never rise on hover. Only event blocks lift (1px, over 150ms, to the lifted shadow) on hover, as the pieces of the week itself, and only where a fine pointer can hover ((hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)), so a tap never leaves a block stuck raised.

**The Rings Inside Rule.** State and highlight outlines on event blocks sit inside the block (negative offset), never outside it. Keyboard focus is the one ring outside: the 2px ink outline offset 2px, raised above the neighbouring lanes. A block with a state ring keeps it, and its focus ring is a shadow outside the block (a 2px sheet gap, then 2px ink), so a focused clash still shows red.

**The Hairline in the Dark Rule.** In dark mode every floating surface (dialog, menu, tooltip, drawer) gets a 1px hairline edge, since shadows cannot read on ink.

## Shapes

Two shape families and nothing between them: everything pressable or countable is a full pill (999px), including buttons, inputs, selects, tags, badges, swatches, the season track and the brand mark; containers are soft rectangles stepping 28px (planner sheet, dialogs; the phone sheet radius on phones, including the rail bottom sheet), 20px (menus, drawer, empty state), 14px (callouts, tooltips, option rows), 10px (menu items). Event blocks sit at 12px, both on the 2D week and as rounded 3D slabs. Course state swatches are 12px circles: filled when selected, a thick ring when some meetings are hidden, dashed when under review.

## Components

### Buttons
Pills that press, never lift.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 38px minimum height (44px under a coarse pointer), no border.
- **Default:** a flat `--fill` (hover `--fill-2`), no shadow, 13px weight 550 label with an optional 16px Tabler stroke icon. The refresh button in the sheet's toolbar is a default pill at 44px, not ink.
- **Primary:** flat ink fill with sheet-coloured text; with a fine pointer, hover mixes 20% neon pink into the ink.
- **Quiet:** transparent; hover is the `--fill` wash.
- **Hero CTA and rail button:** 52px ink pills; the rail button carries a pink count badge and rises in only once the sheet docks. Both take the primary hover.
- **Coloured hovers** (the pink mix on ink pills, the pink and red fills on a rail row's add button) apply only under (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine), so a tap never leaves one stuck; neutral washes hover everywhere.
- **Active / Focus:** scale 0.96 on press; a 2px ink focus outline offset 2px, the same on event blocks, checkboxes and the theme select.

### Chips
- **Tags and badges:** pills on `surface-3` with ink-2 text at 12px weight 600; success, warning and danger variants use the soft fill and the state colour with an icon.
- **Credit tag:** a small ink pill (for ECTS) on each course row.
- **Legend items:** pills on `surface-2` with an 18px colour swatch that doubles as a colour picker.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 28px for the sheet and dialogs, 20px for menus and the drawer, 14px for callouts.
- **Background:** `surface` for raised, `surface-2` for inset callouts and source rows.
- **Shadow Strategy:** floating plus highlight for surfaces that float (see Elevation); none for inset containers.
- **Border:** none on inset containers; floating ones are transparent in light, hairline in dark. Warning and danger callouts are the soft fill alone; a warning callout leads with a dot, a danger one with the warning icon.
- **Checks panel:** below the week, a `surface-2` panel (20px corners) holding the disclosure. Its findings appear without an entrance, so adding or removing a course while it is open never sets them moving.
- **Internal Padding:** 28px dialogs, 11-16px callouts.
- **Empty state:** an inset `surface-2` panel (20px corners, no border, no wash) with a tiny week glyph on a small flat `surface` card: five 16px day columns, one 3px-cornered block placed in each at its own hour (one of them pink, today), dropping in from above one after another, above the title and two lines of help.
- **Dialogs in motion:** on the desktop they come forward out of the page on the ease-out (280ms from 90px behind it, 14px lower and tipped back 5 degrees under 1100px perspective, the backdrop fading in over 200ms; phone bottom sheets slide up in 380ms on `cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1)`). Closing, whether by a close button, Escape or a finished action, runs back the way they came: a fade with an 8px drop and 0.98 scale over 160ms on the ease-out, the frosted backdrop fading with it; phone bottom sheets slide back down (200ms). Instant under reduced motion.
- **Update toast:** when a new version is published (sw.js has already cached its files), a pill on `surface` with the floating shadow floats 10px under the header: "Υπάρχει νέα έκδοση." with a primary "Ανανέωση" and a quiet close. It eases in from above (280ms on the ease-out, from 10px higher at 0.96 scale) and, when closed, leaves upward the way it came (200ms); print and the HTML backup leave it out.
- **Update report:** after a refresh or an import, a collapsed "Τι άλλαξε · N αλλαγές" card on `surface-2` sits right under the notice and lists what changed against the previous data, grouped as changed, added and removed meetings and catalog changes (season, code and name in ink, the detail in ink-2). The next notice hides it; print and the HTML backup leave it out.

### Event Block (signature)
The course as the print of its 3D tile: a 12px block that is a flat field of the full course hue, with no lip, rim, inner edge or shadow at rest; time on top (12px 600), title, then room and teachers at 12px (co-teachers joined by commas), all at full strength in the block's text colour. Where a fine pointer can hover, hover lifts 1px to the lifted shadow over 150ms; touch never lifts. A press scales it to 0.985, gentler than the controls' 0.96 because blocks are wide. Keyboard focus draws the 2px ink outline offset 2px outside the block, above its neighbours; a block with a state ring keeps that ring, and its focus ring is drawn outside as a shadow (a 2px sheet gap, then 2px ink), so a focused clash still shows red. Every ring is drawn inside the block's edge, so packed lanes never overlap their neighbours: review is a 2px dashed amber ring plus a 10px hatched strip down the right edge, kept clear of the text; conflict is a 2px red ring with a light inner band in the sheet colour, plus a warning icon. Hovering a rail row rings every block of that course with a 3px ink ring. The conflict trace pulses its red ring inward. When an added course creates a clash, only the block that was already on the week pulses, once and inward (ring offset -2px to -6px and back over 400ms on the there-and-back curve), timed to the newcomer's landing (600ms plus stagger after a flight, 150ms plus stagger after a dealt block); the newcomer never pulses. New blocks are dealt in Monday to Friday, 40ms apart (at most twelve steps), each pressing from scale 1.1 to size about its centre as it fades in, its floating shadow coming away as it lies flat (240ms on the ease-out); a course flying in from the list is revealed the same way at the flight's landing (600ms). A removed block lifts away where it stood (180ms: the fade on a plain ease so it stays readable, a 1.04 scale on the ease-out), on the desktop grid only, and not when a neighbour widens into its lane or when the week becomes empty; a season switch lifts every old block away this way. While the 3D week stands in for the planner's blocks (mid-scroll, blocks below full opacity, no focus inside the week), the 2D blocks do not animate at all, with no press-in, lift-away, flight or clash pulse: a season switch, or a course added or removed mid-scroll, lands in 3D alone. Clicking grows the course dialog out of the block (400ms on the ease-out).

### Hero Week (scroll morph)
The morph follows the scroll on a critically damped spring rather than 1:1: a slow scroll is tracked closely, while a fast or back-and-forth one plays at the motion's own pace (about four tenths of a second end to end, no reversal on a jitter) and always ends where the page is. The crossfade to the planner's own blocks is eased too, so it never pops when a deal or a throw ends.

A Three.js board (near-white or graphite, with hairline edges) carrying the student's courses as glossy slabs, or chrome slabs while the week is empty. Its five equal day columns match the DOM grid. The lens is wide in the hero (a 36 degree field of view, so near slabs loom and far ones fall away) and closes to 24 degrees as the board lands on the planner, the camera backing off to match: a dolly zoom, so the board keeps its size while its perspective drains out, and the landing stays exact. In the hero the camera is framed to the poster: on wide screens the board's free box starts 2% of the width past the end of the title's short second line and half-way down its long first line (on screen the board is a diamond, so its top corner stands clear of that line's end), runs out past the right edge (to 4% beyond it) and down to 95% of the hero's height, so the sheet's edge cuts the board, and the board's radius fills 70% of that box; on phones the box is the full width between the title and the facts. Slab height encodes how often a course meets per week: 0.18 world units plus 0.22 per extra weekly meeting, capped at five. A once-a-week course is nearly flat; three or more meetings stand clearly tall. The sample week varies by its own counts. **Free view.** The hero board can be explored by hand:
- **Orbit:** the board behaves like a weighted object. Grabbing it catches and stops a spin. While held it follows the hand on a stiff spring, with a slight lag and overshoot. - **Reset and cursors:** double-click resets the view. The cursor is grab, then grabbing while dragging.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** make primary actions ink pills; keep hot pink to small marks (full stop, today, counts, hover).
- **Do** give every pressable thing a full pill shape and the 0.96 press.
- **Do** let free-view interactions (orbit, zoom) yield to scroll and to the copy: plain wheel scrolls, the board springs home on scroll, and it never covers the headline.
- **Do** pick block text colour by contrast (ink or white) for any course hue, including user-chosen ones, measured against the fill the text sits on (the dark-theme mix in dark mode).
- **Do** pair every success, warning and danger state with an icon, dashes or an outline.
- **Do** use the spring easing (`linear()` with ~5% overshoot, fallback cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)) for things that settle into a new value or slot (digits, the season thumb, a block shifting lanes), the ease-out (cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)) for the 2D press-in and for exits, and the there-and-back curve (cubic-bezier(.77,0,.175,1)) only for one-shot pulses that return to rest; make every animation, view transition, smooth scroll and 3D loop disappear under reduced motion.
- **Do** deal tiles and blocks onto the week Monday to Friday (by day, then start time), 40ms apart and the whole deal held to about half a second (bigger weeks close the gap), with one landing per change: in 3D an arrival from the viewer's side of the glass that accelerates under gravity and ends in a squash, never a spring that overshoots into the board; in 2D a press from scale 1.1 to size about the block's centre, with a fade and the floating shadow coming away as the block lies flat (240ms on the ease-out). While the 3D week stands in for the planner's blocks, the change lands in 3D alone.
- **Do** make exits shorter than their entrances (160-200ms) and send things out the way they came in, on the ease-out; the one exception is a removed block, whose opacity runs on the CSS `ease` keyword so the fade stays readable while its scale stays on the ease-out. For the opposite direction use mirrored keyframes, never `animation-direction: reverse`, which turns the ease-out into an ease-in.
- **Do** keep five equal day columns and step narrow lanes down (time and name, time and code, code, number) rather than widening a busy day.
- **Do** keep the week fully visible without horizontal scroll at every width, and keep print as a dedicated A4 landscape layout.
- **Do** keep the 3D week and the planner's week the same object: same course colours, the same five equal columns and lanes, one hand-over sequence with never two hour axes on screen.
- **Do** use tabular figures and display-scale numerals for the week's totals.
- **Do** keep the first screen a poster: the title at poster size at the top left on the page margin (`--gutter`), the facts and the one action at the foot just above the sheet's edge, the week in the space between and beside them, never behind the copy.
- **Do** keep grounds and blocks flat: the studio and the sheet are two flat tones meeting at a seam, and an event block is a field of its course colour.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second accent colour or fill buttons, panels or headers with neon pink.
- **Don't** use grey, pure green, amber or red as a course colour.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface or uppercase tracked labels; Geologica in sentence case carries every role.
- **Don't** use square or small-radius buttons; nothing pressable sits between pill and rectangle.
- **Don't** lift controls on hover or use hard offset shadows; controls press, only event blocks lift.
- **Don't** draw state or highlight rings outside an event block.
- **Don't** tint the neutrals cool or warm; the studio greys are neutral.
- **Don't** put a gradient, glow, shadow or top line between the studio and the sheet, or a lip, rim, inner edge or resting shadow on an event block.
