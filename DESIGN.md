# Design System: Η εβδομάδα μου · ΤΗΜΜΥ

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Week in the Studio"**

The student's own timetable is a product shot. It sits as glossy 3D slabs on a floating board in a neutral photo studio, lit like an object on a seamless backdrop, and the planner sheet rises over it like a page slid onto the set. Everything around the object stays quiet: studio greys, ink type, one neon accent. The colour lives in the courses themselves.

Below the fold the studio becomes a calm, editorial work surface. The week is the canvas and is never cropped sideways. Pills for everything you press, soft 20-28px surfaces, 12px event blocks, giant grotesk numerals for the stats. The signature move is a scroll morph: on desktop and tablet the 3D board swings to a top-down view and lands exactly on the planner's week, and the slabs become its flat blocks. Elsewhere motion is physical and springy: things land with a small overshoot, digits roll, a course flies on an arc into its slot, and the season switch turns the week in 3D. Every one of these collapses to an instant, static path under reduced motion.

The world is modelled on butter.video, chosen by the user. Rejected and not to return: the cool-grey, electric-blue, pastel-course "premium product app" world set in Commissioner with 16/10 radii.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Neutral studio greys with an off-white (light) or ink (dark) planner sheet; no tinted neutrals.
- Ink is the primary action colour; hot pink is a single neon note, never a fill for large surfaces.
- Nine vivid course hues, full-strength on event blocks, with ink or white text chosen by contrast.
- Geologica everywhere, tabular figures by default, display sizes with tight negative tracking.
- Pill controls, soft rectangles for surfaces, 12px for event blocks.
- Soft layered shadows in light mode; hairlines take over in dark mode.
- The hero's 3D week and the planner's 2D week are one object: scrolling morphs one into the other.
- Spring motion with a ~5% overshoot; everything static under reduced motion.

## Colors

A monochrome studio with one neon voice; all saturation is reserved for courses.

### Primary
- **Neon Hot Pink** (`neon-pink`, dark `dark-neon-pink`): the headline's full stop, today's day pill, the today column wash (5% mix), the course "add" hover, the rail button's count badge, link hover underlines, selection and caret. Text on it is always ink (`on-accent`). Its hover step is `neon-pink-hover`.

### Neutral
- **Studio Grey** (`studio-grey` / `dark-studio-grey`): page ground behind the hero and around the sheet.
- **Studio Floor and Glow** (`studio-floor`, `studio-glow` and dark pair): the hero's vertical gradient and the radial key-light pool behind the 3D board.
- **Planner Sheet** (`planner-sheet` / `dark-planner-sheet`): the sheet that rises over the hero; also the text colour on ink pills.
- **Surfaces** (`surface`, `surface-2`, `surface-3` and dark counterparts): raised cards, dialogs and menus (`surface`); input wells, segmented tracks and callouts (`surface-2`); tags and skeleton peaks (`surface-3`).
- **Hairlines** (`hairline`, `hairline-strong`): 1px dividers between rail rows, day columns and stat cells; strong hairlines for button borders and unchecked swatches.
- **Ink** (`ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`): text, primary buttons, the brand mark, the season thumb, focus rings. Ink-3 is the floor for meta text.

### Course palette
Nine vivid hues assigned per course by default, cycling without repeats among selected courses: cobalt, hot pink, tangerine, violet, aqua, lemon, sky, orchid, periwinkle. Blocks use the hue at full strength with a white top sheen; in dark mode the hue is mixed 90% with the surface. Text on a block is ink or white, whichever gives higher WCAG contrast against the hue. In the 3D hero the same hues are darkened to 90% lightness under a full clearcoat; as the board lands, each slab top shifts to its block's exact flat fill so the hand-over is seamless. An empty week shows chrome sample slabs.

### Semantic
- **Success** (passed course), **Warning** (needs review), **Danger** (overlap). Each has a soft companion at 7-12% alpha for callout fills.

### Named Rules
**The One Neon Rule.** Hot pink is the only accent and it appears as small marks (a full stop, a day pill, a count, a hover), never as a button fill or a section background. Primary actions are ink.

**The Colour Is the Course Rule.** Saturation belongs to the course palette. Chrome stays grey and ink; no course is ever grey, and no course hue is pure green, amber or red, so courses can never be mistaken for a state. One exception, accepted by the user: the liquid glass on the header pill and the hero tooltip saturates what lies beneath it (1.8), so a course passing under the frost reads as slightly richer colour, never as chrome colour. Over a cobalt slab the tooltip shows a faint blue with legible text; a coloured slab under the header pill is untested.

**The Never Colour Alone Rule.** Every semantic state carries a second signal: passed is a filled green swatch plus a check icon; review is a dashed amber outline plus a hatch; overlap is a solid red outline plus a warning icon.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geologica (self-hosted Greek and Latin subsets, variable 300-800), with Noto Sans and system-ui as fallback
**Body Font:** Geologica
**Label/Mono Font:** Geologica; a monospace face appears only in the transfer-code textarea

**Character:** One geometric grotesk with Greek built in, used at every size. Display sizes are heavy-ish and tightly tracked; working sizes stay plain and small, so the numbers and the headline do the shouting.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (620, clamp(52px, 7.2vw, 96px), 0.98, -0.04em): the two-line hero headline only, revealed line by line from masks. **Display phone** takes over at 860px and below.
- **Stat** (560, clamp(40px, 4.4vw, 66px), 1.2, -0.04em): the four week totals, rendered as odometer digits; **Stat phone** (38px) at 860px and below.
- **Headline** (600, 24px, 1.2, -0.03em): dialog titles.
- **Title large** (600, 22px, 1.2, -0.025em): the empty-state title.
- **Title** (600, 20px, 1.25, -0.025em): panel headings (course rail, weekly schedule).
- **Title small** (600, 15px, 1.3): the checks summary and dialog subheads.
- **Lede** (400, clamp(16px, 1.3vw, 19px), 1.5): the hero sub-sentence, held to 31ch.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.5; paragraphs 1.6): default text with tabular figures.
- **Control** (550, 13px, 1.3): buttons, selects, menu items, the season switch.
- **Event title** (600, 13px, 1.25): course names inside blocks; time lines 11px 600.
- **Meta** (400, 12px): course meta, sync time, footers, tooltips.
- **Label** (600, 11.5px): tags, badges, counters, day-head meta. Sentence case, never uppercase.
- **Print ramp** (print only): title 22px, stats 20px, day names 11px, event titles 9px, event times, rooms and day meta 8px. Teachers are dropped.

### Named Rules
**The Numbers Are the Headline Rule.** Figures are tabular everywhere, and the week's totals are set at display scale, not tucked into small chips.

**The One Family Rule.** Geologica carries every role; hierarchy comes from size, weight and tracking, never from a second display face.

## Layout

A week-first canvas inside a sheet. The hero is a full-viewport sticky studio (100svh, min 600px; 92svh on phones); the planner sheet overlaps it by 13svh (9svh on phones) and carries everything else. Content tracks a 2080px maximum with fluid 2vw gutters (16px on phones).

- **Desktop (>1180px):** a 340px sticky course rail beside the week, divided by a hairline. On wide layouts the rail can be hidden so the week runs full-width, with the floating rail button to bring it back.
- **Tablet (861-1180px):** the week takes the full width; the rail becomes a 340px side drawer floating 12px from the edges.
- **Phone (<=860px):** the week becomes a vertical day-by-day agenda with sticky day headings; the rail becomes a bottom sheet (84dvh, grab handle, scrim); dialogs become bottom sheets; the sheet corners drop to the phone sheet radius.
- A floating ink "Μαθήματα" pill with the selected count opens the rail from anywhere once the planner is in view.

Day headings (name, meta, today pill) are centred in their columns and reserve exactly two lines of meta (2.7em) so every day name aligns. Today's meta puts "Σήμερα" on its own line above "N συναντήσεις · N ώρες". On the phone agenda, headings are a left-aligned row and the word runs inline with a comma. Phones and print drop the two-line reservation.

Spacing runs on 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 22 / 28px; panels breathe at 22px vertical padding, the hour grid is 56px per hour, and the header bar is 76px (68px on phones).

### Named Rules
**The No Sideways Week Rule.** The week never scrolls horizontally. The grid is a 54px hour axis plus five equal day columns, whatever they hold. Parallel lessons split their day into lanes, and narrow lanes step down by container width. At 112px or less a lane shows its start time and the name, set compact with soft hyphens at Greek syllable breaks. At 80px or less, and for lessons of an hour or less, it shows start time plus course code. At 60px or less it shows the code only. At 50px or less the shared ECE prefix drops and only the course number remains. The full name always stays in the tooltip and dialog. Margin notes move beside the week only when the busiest day still gets 150px per lane.

**The Print Is Its Own Layout Rule.** Print is one A4 landscape sheet in the selected theme: chrome hidden, the sheet and blocks exactly as on screen (light or dark), no today marking, the week scaled to fit and forced fully visible, the print type ramp, all motion and shadows off. Nothing on the printed page sizes itself with viewport units, because Chrome's print preview resolves them against the browser window and a window-tall page spills a blank second sheet.

## Elevation & Depth

A hybrid: soft, layered ambient shadows plus a 1px top highlight on raised surfaces in light mode; on ink, where shadows vanish, a hairline marks every raised edge instead. Real depth lives in the hero, where a physically lit 3D board sits on a shadow-catching floor. Lighting is tuned per theme: in light mode a strong key from the upper left with a soft rim, in dark mode a softer key with a stronger cool rim. The floor shadow is 24% in light mode and 60% in dark mode. The board carries hairline edge lines. In light mode it is near-white and clearcoated; in dark mode it is graphite, rougher, with little clearcoat. The 3D stage is its own fixed layer above the planner sheet. It is clipped away over the sheet's toolbar and stats band and over the docked rail, so the board slides under the planner's chrome. Frosted glass is reserved for things floating over content: the header pill (20px blur, 1.8 saturate), the hero tooltip (12px blur, 1.8 saturate) and the dialog backdrop (8px blur). The two floating surfaces, the header pill and the hero tooltip, are liquid glass: the event blocks' top sheen, and a rim lit from the upper left (a bright 1px top-left crescent, a faint lower-right line, a lens shade along the inner bottom and a soft inner edge glow) in place of the plain top highlight. The rim is a plain-CSS approximation; true refraction needs SVG filters in backdrop-filter, which only Chromium supports. Under reduced transparency both turn solid surface with no blur. The dialog backdrop stays plain frost with no rim.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Rest** (`0 1px 2px #0e0e0e12, 0 1px 1px #0e0e0e0a`): default buttons and event blocks.
- **Lifted** (`0 12px 28px -12px #0e0e0e2e, 0 2px 6px -2px #0e0e0e14`): ink pills, the season thumb, hovered event blocks, tooltips.
- **Floating** (`0 32px 64px -24px #0e0e0e40, 0 6px 16px -6px #0e0e0e17`): dialogs, menus, the rail drawer, the rail button.
- **Highlight** (`inset 0 1px 0 #ffffffcc`): paired with lifted and floating surfaces.
- **Glass rim** (light `inset 1px 1px 0 #ffffff, inset -1px -1px 0 #ffffff40, inset 0 -10px 16px -12px #0e0e0e14, inset 0 0 14px -6px #ffffffb3`; dark `inset 1px 1px 0 #ffffff47, inset -1px -1px 0 #ffffff1a, inset 0 -10px 16px -12px #00000066, inset 0 0 14px -6px #ffffff1f`): the header pill and the hero tooltip only, replacing the highlight there. It travels with the **glass sheen** (`linear-gradient(180deg, #ffffff66, #ffffff00 46%)`; dark `#ffffff26`, the event blocks' stop and dark strength) and, on the header pill, the **glass fill** (surface at 64% in light so the sheen and lit edge read, 72% in dark); the tooltip keeps its 86% surface fill and, in dark, its hairline.
- **Sheet rise** (`0 -30px 60px -34px #0e0e0e59`): under the planner sheet's top edge where it overlaps the hero.

### Named Rules
**The Press, Don't Lift Rule.** Controls press (scale 0.96 on active) and never rise on hover. Only event blocks lift (2px) on hover, because they are objects on the board.

**The Rings Inside Rule.** State and highlight outlines on event blocks sit inside the block (negative offset), never outside it.

**The Hairline in the Dark Rule.** In dark mode every raised surface (dialog, menu, tooltip, drawer, sheet) gets a 1px hairline edge, since shadows cannot read on ink.

## Shapes

Two shape families and nothing between them: everything pressable or countable is a full pill (999px), including buttons, inputs, selects, tags, badges, swatches, the season track and the brand mark; containers are soft rectangles stepping 28px (planner sheet, dialogs; the phone sheet radius on phones, including the rail bottom sheet), 20px (menus, drawer, empty state), 14px (callouts, tooltips, option rows), 10px (menu items). Event blocks sit at 12px, both on the 2D week and as rounded 3D slabs. Course state swatches are 12px circles: filled when selected, a thick ring when some meetings are hidden, dashed when under review.

## Components

### Buttons
Pills that press, never lift.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 38px minimum height, 1px strong hairline border.
- **Default:** surface fill, rest shadow, 13px weight 550 label with an optional 16px Tabler stroke icon.
- **Primary:** ink fill with sheet-coloured text and the lifted shadow; hover mixes 20% neon pink into the ink.
- **Quiet:** transparent; hover is a 7% ink wash.
- **Hero CTA and rail button:** 52px ink pills; the rail button carries a pink count badge and rises in only once the sheet docks.
- **Active / Focus:** scale 0.96 on press; a 2px ink focus outline offset 2px.

### Chips
- **Tags and badges:** pills on `surface-3` with ink-2 text at 11.5px weight 600; success, warning and danger variants use the soft fill and the state colour with an icon.
- **Credit tag:** a small ink pill (for ECTS) on each course row.
- **Legend items:** pills on `surface-2` with an 18px colour swatch that doubles as a colour picker.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 28px for the sheet and dialogs, 20px for menus and the drawer, 14px for callouts.
- **Background:** `surface` for raised, `surface-2` for inset callouts and source rows.
- **Shadow Strategy:** floating plus highlight for raised surfaces (see Elevation).
- **Border:** transparent in light, hairline in dark; warning and danger callouts use a 24-30% tinted border on the soft fill.
- **Internal Padding:** 28px dialogs, 11-16px callouts.
- **Update toast:** when a new version is published (sw.js has already cached its files), a pill on `surface` with the lifted shadow floats 10px under the header: "Υπάρχει νέα έκδοση." with a primary "Ανανέωση" and a quiet close. It springs in from above; print and the HTML backup leave it out.
- **Update report:** after a refresh or an import, a collapsed "Τι άλλαξε · N αλλαγές" card on `surface` sits right under the notice and lists what changed against the previous data, grouped as changed, added and removed meetings and catalog changes (season, code and name in ink, the detail in ink-2). The next notice hides it; print and the HTML backup leave it out.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 40px pill well on `surface-2` with a hairline border (44px with a leading icon for search).
- **Focus:** border turns ink, a 3px 14%-ink ring appears, and the well brightens to `surface`.
- **Checkboxes:** native, accent-coloured ink.

### Navigation
- **Header:** a fixed frosted pill floating 10px inside the viewport over the hero (brand mark, quiet profile, theme control, data menu, print). The theme control is one quiet pill: a leading icon that follows the choice (sun, moon, or sun-moon for system), the label and an inline chevron. As the sheet reaches it, a scroll-linked solid sheet-coloured bar fades in beneath it and it docks. On phones labels drop to 40px icon pills.
- **Season switch:** a segmented pill track whose ink thumb springs under the active season; switching turns the week on its vertical axis with a view transition.

### Event Block (signature)
The course as an object: a 12px block filled with the full course hue, a white top sheen, a 1px inner ink edge and the rest shadow; time on top (11px 600), title, then room and teacher at reduced opacity. Hover lifts 2px to the lifted shadow. Every ring is drawn inside the block's edge, so packed lanes never overlap their neighbours: review is a 2px dashed amber ring plus a hatch; conflict is a 2px red ring with a light inner band in the sheet colour, plus a warning icon. Hovering a rail row rings every block of that course with a 3px ink ring. The conflict trace pulses its red ring inward. Clicking grows the course dialog out of the block.

### Course Rail Row
A three-column row (12px state swatch, title and meta, 32px round add button) divided by hairlines. Adding a course flashes the row pink, pops the swatch, and flies a block on an arc from the swatch into each new slot of the week.

### Stats
Four cells divided by hairlines, a 12.5px label over a stat-scale odometer number whose digits spring to their new value with a 40ms stagger; overlaps turn the label and number danger red.

### Hero Week (scroll morph)
A Three.js board (near-white or graphite, with hairline edges) carrying the student's courses as glossy slabs, or chrome slabs while the week is empty. Its five equal day columns match the DOM grid. Slab height encodes how often a course meets per week: 0.18 world units plus 0.22 per extra weekly meeting, capped at five. A once-a-week course is nearly flat; three or more meetings stand clearly tall. The sample week varies by its own counts. Each real slab has its course code engraved, centred, into its top: a groove in the slab's own colour, darker the deeper it goes, matte with no clearcoat, and walls drawn as a normal map from the lettering so they catch the scene's light and shift as the board turns. Narrow tops shrink the code, dropping the ECE prefix last; a top too small stays plain. The board marks hours with lines and half hours with fainter dashed lines. All words on the board (day labels, hours, engraved codes) fade together over e .35-.65, before the planner's own text arrives. Loading: while the data loads, the planner shows shimmering skeletons of its course rows, stats, day heads and blocks; until the 3D board is live, the hero shows a skeleton of the board in its pose (the same shimmer on a plane in the surface colour), which fades as the board rises in, and only where the 3D can load (html.has-3d), so it never waits forever. Rendering budget: the board draws at the display rate only while something moves (scroll, pointer, drag, springs); with only the slow drift and conflict pulse left it draws at 30 fps, which looks identical, and docked under the planner it draws nothing until a scroll, resize or the drawer wakes it. It starts in short tasks with shaders compiled asynchronously, and canvases behind uploaded textures are freed. In the hero it drifts gently, follows the cursor, shows a frosted tooltip on hover and opens the course dialog on click.

**Free view.** The hero board can be explored by hand:
- **Orbit:** the board behaves like a weighted object. Grabbing it catches and stops a spin. While held it follows the hand on a stiff spring, with a slight lag and overshoot. Released, it keeps the hand's speed, soft-limited so a heavy board never whips round: a harder push still goes further, but even a violent flick turns it less than half a turn. It then comes to a firm stop under drag plus a constant grip, in under a second, instead of creeping. Yaw is free. Past its pitch band, tilt stretches like a rubber band and springs back, and the camera never flips over the top.
- **Feel:** while held the board lifts slightly, then drops back on release with one small settle, not a bounce train. It banks into a spin and nods with a tilt. Slabs lean like jelly, more for taller and outer slabs, and squash as the board lands. All of this fades out through the morph. Under reduced motion a drag sets the pose directly: no throw, lift, settle or jelly.
- **Zoom:** pinch or Ctrl+wheel zooms between 0.8x and 1.6x. A plain wheel always scrolls the page.
- **Reset and cursors:** double-click resets the view. The cursor is grab, then grabbing while dragging.
- **Touch:** horizontal swipes spin the board and vertical swipes scroll.
- **Click vs drag:** a 5px threshold separates the two, so a click on a slab still opens the course.

Free view lives only at the top of the page (scroll progress at most 0.002): grab, double-click reset and pinch zoom start nowhere else, and a hand still holding the board when the page scrolls lets go to the homing spring. As soon as the page scrolls, the orbit springs home and its offsets fade with the landing, so the morph always lands exactly. A press that travels more than 5px is never a click on a course. A clearance pan keeps the headline, sub and CTA in front at any orbit or zoom: the board's projected outline is kept 4px right of the copy (12px above it on phones), on a spring that fades out as the board lands. The untouched home pose needs no pan. Above 860px, scrolling from the hero to the docked planner morphs it into the planner's week. The camera swings to straight down, the board scales onto the live rectangle of the planner's day columns, and the slabs flatten. The hand-over runs strictly in sequence: the 3D labels fade first, the board and edges dissolve, then the slab tops shift to their blocks' flat fill. Next the planner's own grid chrome (day heads, hour axis, lines, column borders) fades in. Last, the real blocks with their text crossfade in over the final tenth of the scroll. Once docked, the stage is hidden and rendering stops; scrolling up reverses the morph. On phones the board tilts away under the rising sheet. Under reduced motion it is a still picture that scrolls away. Focus inside the week forces the 2D week visible and hides the stage; print does the same, and so does an open rail drawer or phone sheet, so the 3D layer never draws over it.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** make primary actions ink pills; keep hot pink to small marks (full stop, today, counts, hover).
- **Do** give every pressable thing a full pill shape and the 0.96 press.
- **Do** let free-view interactions (orbit, zoom) yield to scroll and to the copy: plain wheel scrolls, the board springs home on scroll, and it never covers the headline.
- **Do** pick block text colour by contrast (ink or white) for any course hue, including user-chosen ones.
- **Do** pair every success, warning and danger state with an icon, dashes or an outline.
- **Do** use the spring easing (`linear()` with ~5% overshoot, fallback cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)) for things that land, and make every animation, view transition, smooth scroll and 3D loop disappear under reduced motion.
- **Do** keep five equal day columns and step narrow lanes down (time and name, time and code, code, number) rather than widening a busy day.
- **Do** keep the week fully visible without horizontal scroll at every width, and keep print as a dedicated A4 landscape layout.
- **Do** keep the 3D week and the planner's week the same object: same course colours, the same five equal columns and lanes, one hand-over sequence with never two hour axes on screen.
- **Do** use tabular figures and display-scale numerals for the week's totals.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second accent colour or fill buttons, panels or headers with neon pink.
- **Don't** use grey, pure green, amber or red as a course colour.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface or uppercase tracked labels; Geologica in sentence case carries every role.
- **Don't** use square or small-radius buttons; nothing pressable sits between pill and rectangle.
- **Don't** lift controls on hover or use hard offset shadows; controls press, only event blocks lift.
- **Don't** draw state or highlight rings outside an event block.
- **Don't** tint the neutrals cool or warm; the studio greys are neutral.
